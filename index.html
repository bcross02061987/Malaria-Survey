How it works

Each phone saves responses on that phone only. Export a backup file from every collector, then import those files on one phone and export the combined Excel workbook.

Important: Do not clear browser data or uninstall the app before exporting a backup. This app does not send household data to a server.

New household survey

Enter household details, then complete the individual questions for every family member. The Head of Family is included as Member 1.

Before starting, explain the purpose of the survey and obtain consent. Record the name and Questions 4–13 for every family member, including the Head of Family. Keep exported files secure and share them only with authorised people.

Responses & data sharing

Export the Excel file for analysis. Use the backup file to transfer and merge data from other collectors.

Imported responses are merged. Duplicate survey IDs are skipped.
